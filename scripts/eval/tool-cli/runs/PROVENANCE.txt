==============================

grade.py digest was 1e58699b4cdf1333692b0f23515e73b4f89e3d49925ad3d52cbeef66ce082440.

harness is a NEW commit A'.

SPEC.txt section 6 is explicit about what that means: "No prompt/grader/table
edits after commit A — if one is needed, the matrix restarts from zero under a
new commit A'." These results therefore do NOT carry forward as results under the
current freeze. They stand as the record of what was run under the old one.

Measured, not assumed: re-grading the surviving E2 raw tree with the FIXED grader
reproduces runs/E2-20260828T090026Z/summary.json with zero differing keys, and
rendering that regrade reproduces its report.txt byte for byte. So the fix is
result-invariant on the data that still exists — evidence about what a re-run
would be expected to show, not a substitute for the re-run SPEC section 6
requires. E1 (20260828T063955Z) cannot be re-graded at all: its raw run tree was
in a worktree that has since been released.
